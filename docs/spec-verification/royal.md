# Royal/Sword verification notes

The scenarios in `cpp/tests/wb_spec/royal.cpp` use card text from the versioned official Japanese rotation catalog at `data/cards/svwb_rotation_ja.json`. Each TSV row links to that card's official card detail page. The existing investigation notes at `docs/仕様調査.md` provide general battle-rule context; the cases here assert card-specific outcomes from the printed text.

The 42 added cases exercise summon counts and token identity, enhancement thresholds, stat and keyword grants, evolution/super-evolution, Rally, modes, selected and area damage, generated cards, Evidence Act, deck draws, last words, amulet triggers, and flag countdown. The Evidence cases also cover the official rule that a new Evidence amulet banishes existing Evidence amulets and combines their clue counts ([Cygames glossary](https://shadowverse-wb.com/ja/deck/build_edit/?battle_format=1&bk_u_battle_format=1&bk_u_class=3&bk_u_key=deck_build&class=3)). Twenty cases were added after the first run using captured official token records in `cpp/tests/wb_spec/official_supplement.json`. Existing `test_wb.cpp` and the other spec suites also cover Royal faith, evidence hand swaps/copies, crests, and token smoke checks.

The first-run log reported setup/assertion failures in early cases. Auditing against official text corrected automatic Enhance payment/action choices, required enemy target slots, Last Words outputs that leave Knights or Evidence, the Captain's Steel Knights' 2/2 printed stats, and checks that had treated Storm (疾走) as Rush (突進). In the second run, the remaining three failures were likewise fixture expectations: Barbaros is a follower so playing it does not trigger the Flag's Spell counter (a countdown-7 Flag becomes 2 after its fanfare), Quick Blade has Storm, and Evidence Act consumes one clue from the original Evidence before the spell creates another. Those expectations are updated. None of the observed failures establish a production bug. The parent task owns the next build/run.

Remaining Royal branches not specifically asserted here include Maid's Practice deck return/draw filtering, discard-random-damage, spell-triggered effects, additional turn-end random effects, evidence hand-stat swaps and copy variants, crest expiry rewards, and several late-set enhancement/board-state combinations. The card catalog contains additional direct card checks; these cases prioritize effects with deterministic setups and observable outcomes.
